Help & Instructions

  • Record Game Score: Click the button at the top or press Ctrl+` to open the score modal.
  • Edit Game: Click a game number in the grid to edit scores for that game.
  • Add/Remove Players: Use the menu to add new players or remove inactive ones. Minimum 4 players required.
  • Swap Players: Drag and drop active/inactive players to swap positions.
  • Export CSV: Use the menu to export all scores to a CSV file.
  • Clear Scores/Players: Use the menu to reset scores or remove all players and games.
